Inside Every Capsule

8 Gut Actives, Researched and Purposeful

Each ingredient in Villora's 695 mg proprietary blend was chosen for a specific role in digestive health — not for filler or flavoring.

1
Psyllium Husk
A soluble fiber derived from Plantago ovata seeds. Absorbs water to form a gel, softening stool and supporting regularity. One of the most researched dietary fibers for digestive function.
2
Oat Bran
Rich in beta-glucan — a soluble fiber recognized by the FDA for heart-health claims — and supports slow, steady digestion.
3
Flax Seed
Provides both soluble and insoluble fiber plus omega-3 fatty acids (ALA). Insoluble fiber adds bulk to stool, while mucilaginous compounds ease transit.
4
Apple Pectin
A gel-forming soluble fiber from apple cell walls. Pectin acts as a prebiotic, feeding beneficial bacteria and contributing to a healthy gut lining environment.
5
Konjac Root
Contains glucomannan — an exceptionally viscous soluble fiber that expands in the digestive tract, promoting fullness and regular bowel movements.
6
Plum Powder
Prunes and plums are a traditional remedy for mild constipation, now backed by research showing sorbitol and fiber content supports gut motility.
7
Green Black Walnut Hull
The outer hull of the black walnut (Juglans nigra) has been used in traditional herbal medicine as a digestive cleanser. Contains juglone and tannins. Note: contains tree nut.
8
Lactobacillus acidophilus (1.5B CFU)
One of the most studied probiotic strains. Supports the balance of gut bacteria, aids lactose digestion and complements the prebiotic effect of the fiber blend. 1.5 billion CFU guaranteed at manufacture.

How is Villora different from other fiber supplements?
Most fiber supplements are single-fiber powders (just psyllium or just inulin). Villora combines 6 distinct fiber types — soluble, insoluble, gel-forming and prebiotic — plus a probiotic and walnut hull, all in one capsule you simply swallow. No mixing, no gritty texture, no measuring.
When will I start noticing results?
Results vary by individual. Some customers report feeling a difference within the first week; others find it takes 2–4 weeks for their gut to adjust to a new fiber routine. Consistency is key — taking Villora daily with a full glass of water gives the best chance of seeing results.
